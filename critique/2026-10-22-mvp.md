# Critic pass 1: MVP (Chapter 1), 2026-10-22

A fresh-eyes playtest by the indie-critic agent (`.claude/agents/indie-critic.md`), run on a build
snapshot with real keyboard and mouse input through Playwright and a fake clock, so standing still cost
1–3 s of thinking time like a human. It played 1-1 from a fresh save and the first five minutes, then
1-2 (one death), 1-4 and the 1-8 boss. It couldn't judge sound. Its screenshots were working files and
aren't kept.

At the end: what was done about each point, and how it was checked.

## Verdict

The frozen frame is good: red aim lines, desk lanes and "!" tells read instantly. But once you hold a
gun, the fights have no tension. Gunners are free kills during their tell, trading shots turns into
accidental parries, and par never fails. The worst problem is a bug: the tally screen ignores mouse
clicks, so every clear ends on a screen that looks frozen.

## Ranked issues

1. **Critical: tally buttons are dead to the mouse.** NEXT LEVEL highlighted on hover, but clicks did
   nothing; only Enter worked. `tally.ts`: `this.shown < r.lines.length + 3` is always true, so the
   "skip the count-up" check ate every click.
2. **Major: gunners are free kills.** No movement in the `aim` state; your bullet covers 121 px during
   the 0.55 s tell and gunners held 64–112 px, so every gunner that started aiming could be shot
   before it fired.
3. **Major: parries are the default result of a shootout, and pay more than kills.** In 1-4 four
   unintended parries paid +12, as much as six kills. Bullets traded at once fly the same line and
   cancel.
4. **Major: punches whiff by a pixel.** Windup at 15 px centre to centre, knockback 7 px to 22, reach
   21: the follow-up punch always missed unless you stepped in. 8 of 15 punches whiffed.
5. **Major: props silently eat shots.** Shots hit desks and copiers near the gun; the aim guide just
   shrank, and bullets drawn 7 px up looked like they cleared low desks.
6. **Major: banners cover the action.** "SHATTERED" sat where the camera keeps you; "WAVE 2" covered
   the player as the wave entered; "STILL./FRAME." blanked the middle of the replay.
7. **Major: the boss has no climax.** The Manager died to its own gunner's stray bullet while a punch
   went to a nearer grunt (the punch picked the nearest red, not the one aimed at). The phase change
   said "WAVE 2"; the damage guard was 8 blinking pixels; no ceremony on death.
8. **Major: par is decorative.** Sloppy first clears beat par every time (12.1/20, 6.2/22, 13.9/24,
   16.5/50).
9. **Major: Stride is a no-op.** The sim moves you 72 px per game second whatever the time scale, so
   slowing time while walking changes nothing in the game. Trigger I saves 0.04 s per shot.
10. **Major: the disarm loop and click overload.** Disarmed guns dropped at the red's feet and it
    re-armed the same tick; clicking a red picked up the gun under it; clicking a pistol just out of
    reach threw your empty gun away.

Minor: sparse shards that fly into walls; invisible stun (1 px); gunners look like grunts from behind;
a double cursor (CSS crosshair plus the game's); a title demo buried under a 50% dither; glass that
reads as thin poles; no pause on blur; corner snags; the 1-8 pistol hidden under a desk monitor; HUD
plates flickering as you walk under them; 26 s and three clicks from page load to the first shatter.

Decisions it would reverse: "aiming doesn't move time" (combined with stationary tells, gunfights were
solved); pars with generous slack; bullets drawn 7 px up (fakes clearance over desks).

Cut list it proposed: Stride as built; Trigger I and Mag I; the map on a fresh save; the "V1.0" label;
the HUD-avoid logic; 1-1 saying the same thing three times.

What's working: the frozen frame; instant restart and load (41 KB gzip JS + a 15 KB atlas); palette
discipline.
