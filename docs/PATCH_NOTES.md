# Patch notes

## 1.0 — all five chapters, the Gauntlet, and new type (2026-10-22)

### Four new chapters, 32 levels

- **Chapter 2, Rush Hour** (the subway): Runners weave in fast; Brutes are regulars now. **Trains**
  run the tracks in game time: the rails light red first, then nothing on them survives, you
  included. Boss: **The Conductor**, who rides the trains and comes down where you stand.
- **Chapter 3, Under Construction** (rooftops): the **SMG** (three rounds a click), **Snipers** that
  hold a red line on you for a second and a half, **ledges** you can punch reds off (and can't fall
  off yourself), and a **crane hook** swinging across the roof. Boss: **The Foreman**, who shrugs off
  bullets and loses a third of itself every time it goes over the edge.
- **Chapter 4, Strobe** (a white nightclub): the **katana** cuts reds and bullets alike; **Mirrors**
  send your bullets straight back. **The beat** lights up part of the dance floor and drops on it;
  some rooms **strobe**, dark between flashes. Boss: **The DJ**, whose beat quickens as it cracks.
- **Chapter 5, The Core** (the white void): **Splitters** break into two Shards; **gravity wells**
  bend bullets; and the rules glitch: **time runs when you stand still**, **every shot echoes**,
  **left is right**. Boss: **The Frame**, whose eyes only open while time runs. Then the ending and
  the credits.
- Every level's intro card names what's new in it.

### Gauntlet

Unlocks after Chapter 2. Endless waves, five to an arena, through five arenas (one from each
chapter), with a glitch in every arena after the first. Every run is the same run. Coins bank as you
go; your best wave shows on the title.

### Clearer to read

All the text was redrawn: two new bitmap faces with real lowercase and bold strokes, sentence case,
dark ink on light paper cards, on their own sharper layer over the game. Tips show your actual keys
as keycaps. The HUD, every menu, the map, the Workshop, the tally and the death strip were rebuilt
around them.

### Changes

- **Heavy reds** (Brutes, bosses) take one punch's stun, then shrug punches off for a moment, and can't
  be knocked back or shouldered aside. Fists no longer stun-lock a Brute or the Manager. The Manager's
  par is looser to match.
- **Workshop:** the SMG and the katana join the loadout.
- **Settings:** "Fewer flashes" now also steadies hazard warnings and turns off the strobe.
- New sounds for trains, the beat, landings, the katana, reflections and more.
- Resting shards now catch the light now and then.
